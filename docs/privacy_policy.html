TravelMate AI — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Your Privacy Matters

TravelMate AI is designed with privacy in mind. Your trips, itineraries, expenses, journal entries, and preferences are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your personal Firebase account. The app uses Google AdMob for advertisements and Firebase for analytics and crash reporting. Third-party providers may collect limited device information according to their own privacy policies.

1. Introduction

UK Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the TravelMate AI mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use TravelMate AI.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your travel data is primarily stored on your device.

  • Trip plans, itineraries, and travel notes
  • Expense records and budget information
  • Packing lists and travel documents (encrypted locally)
  • Journal entries and travel memories
  • Preferences such as theme, language, and display settings
  • Recent searches and AI chat history
  • Cached weather and currency data

3. Firebase Account Sync

TravelMate AI supports optional cloud sync via Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage). If you sign in with Google, the following data may be stored in your personal Firebase account:

  • Trip plans and itineraries
  • Profile information (name, profile photo from Google account)
  • Uploaded travel photos and documents

You control this data. You can delete your account and associated data at any time from within the app settings.

4. AI Travel Assistant

The AI chat and itinerary features send your queries to an AI service to generate travel recommendations, packing suggestions, and budget advice. Queries are processed to generate responses and are not used to build personal profiles. We recommend avoiding sharing sensitive personal or financial details in AI chats.

5. Information Sent to Third Parties

TravelMate AI may send limited technical information when using app features:

  • Google AdMob: Advertising ID, device identifiers, IP address, diagnostic and ad interaction data for displaying advertisements.
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage events to improve app performance and features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash logs and device state to diagnose and fix issues.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Feature flags fetched to control app behaviour.
  • Google Maps: Location coordinates when you use map or nearby-places features.
  • Weather / Currency APIs: Your location or selected currency codes when fetching live data. No personal identifiers are included.

6. Location Data

TravelMate AI may request access to your device location to provide map navigation, weather for your current location, and nearby points of interest. Location data is used in real time and is not stored beyond your current session unless you save a location to a trip plan.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

TravelMate AI displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may use mobile advertising identifiers or similar technologies to display ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob

8. Permissions

Depending on your device and the features you use, the app may request:

  • Internet access (sync, AI features, ads, live data)
  • Location access (maps, weather, nearby places)
  • Camera and photo library access (travel photos, document vault)
  • Notification permission (trip reminders, alerts)
  • Biometric / device credential access (secure document vault unlock)

9. Notifications

TravelMate AI may request notification permission to deliver trip reminders and packing reminders. Notifications are generated locally on your device. You can manage or disable them at any time from your device settings.

10. Document Vault

The encrypted document vault stores travel documents (passport scans, tickets, etc.) locally on your device using AES encryption. Documents are not uploaded to any server unless you explicitly choose to back them up. We strongly recommend protecting your device with a PIN or biometrics.

11. Children's Privacy

TravelMate AI is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising and analytics providers may process limited technical information according to their own policies.

12. Data Security

  • Sensitive documents are encrypted locally using AES encryption.
  • Firebase data is protected by Firebase Security Rules and your Google account credentials.
  • We recommend protecting your device with a PIN, password, or biometrics.
  • Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

13. Third-Party Services

TravelMate AI currently integrates with:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase Auth, Firestore, Storage, FCM (Core services)
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Stability)
  • Firebase Remote Config (Feature flags)
  • Google Maps (Maps and navigation)
  • Weather and currency data APIs
  • AI language model service (Travel assistant)

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies separately.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.

15. Your Rights

  • You can manage or delete your trip data from within the app at any time.
  • You can delete your account and associated Firebase data from app settings.
  • You can clear local app data by uninstalling the app.
  • You can revoke location and other permissions from your device settings.
